Quick Help

How do I track my order?

Go to Track Order, enter your Order ID (e.g. ORD-20260410-001) and click Track. Your delivery will appear on the live map.

When will my order be delivered?

Most orders are delivered within 4–6 hours. Same-day delivery is available if you order before 2 PM.

How do I cancel an order?

Log in to your dashboard, go to My Orders, and select Cancel. Orders can be cancelled before "In Progress" status.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept UPI, Debit/Credit cards, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery.

Is Gas Track available in my city?

We currently serve 12 cities: Delhi, Noida, Gurugram, Mumbai, Pune, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Jaipur, Lucknow, Ahmedabad, and Kolkata.
