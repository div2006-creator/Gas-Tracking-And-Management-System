Our Story

About Gas Track

We're on a mission to make LPG delivery safe, transparent, and accessible to every home and business in India.

Our Mission

To revolutionize LPG delivery in India by making it real-time trackable, fully digital, and customer-first. We believe every household deserves to know exactly when their gas is arriving.

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted LPG delivery platform, powering 10 million deliveries annually across every state — from metro cities to tier-3 towns.

Our Values

Safety first, transparency always, customer obsession, and continuous improvement. We hold ourselves to the highest standards so you never have to worry.

Our Story

Born From a Real Problem

Gas Track started in 2024 when our founder — frustrated by yet another missed LPG delivery — decided to build the platform he wished existed. No calls, no waiting, no uncertainty.

Within months, we built a working prototype for the KCC Hackathon 2026, connecting customers, delivery agents, and admins on a single platform with real-time GPS tracking and a seamless ordering experience.

Today, Gas Track serves 50,000+ households and businesses, with 100,000+ deliveries completed and a 4.9-star average rating.
